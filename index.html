⌨ Emacs Key Dojo

Learn real Emacs keybindings by pressing them — every key here is pulled straight from research-emacs/docs/KEYBOARD.md, individually checked against a real Emacs build.

All five levels are open — jump into whichever you feel like drilling. Level 5 covers every number, symbol, Alt and Ctrl+Alt key, and shows a live mini-buffer so you can watch what each command does.

Play with your real keyboard. Your browser may intercept a few combinations it reserves for itself (e.g. Ctrl+W closing a tab, Ctrl+N opening a window) — if a key seems to do nothing, that is almost always why, not a bug in the game. On a Mac, if Alt/Option combos type accented characters instead, set Option to act as Meta in your browser or OS.
